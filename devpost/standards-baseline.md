# Standards baseline (researched 2026-11-11)

The Lens supports these OSHA construction rules. It does not replace the physical protection they require.

## Crane and rotating equipment: 29 CFR 1926.1424, Work area control
- Applies where the rotating superstructure could strike a worker or pinch or crush them against another object.
- Employer must train workers assigned near the equipment to recognize those struck-by and pinch/crush areas.
- Employer must erect and maintain control lines, warning lines, railings or similar barriers at the boundary of the hazard area.
- Where barriers are not feasible: warning signs (for example "Danger - Swing/Crush Zone") plus high-visibility markings on the equipment, and training on what they mean.
- Before a worker goes into a hazard area the operator cannot see, the operator must be told, and must not rotate until told the worker is clear.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.1424

## Floor holes: 29 CFR 1926.501(b)(4) and 1926.502(i)
- A hole is a gap 2 inches or more in its least dimension in a floor or other walking surface.
- Holes more than 6 feet above a lower level: workers protected by covers, guardrails around the hole, or personal fall arrest.
- Any hole: covers protect against tripping in or stepping into it.
- Covers must meet 1926.502(i); they are color coded or marked (OSHA accepted safety orange in a 2004 letter). Exact wording of (i)(4) to be read in eCFR before quoting it.
- Sources: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.501 and https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/standardinterpretations/1998-11-17

## Signs: 29 CFR 1926.200
- Danger signs: only for immediate hazards. Red upper panel, black border, white lower panel for wording.
- Caution signs: potential hazards or unsafe practices. Yellow background, black panel with yellow lettering.
- Signs stay visible while work is under way and come down when the hazard is gone.
- Source: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1926/1926.200

## Not yet read
- 1926.1425 (keeping clear of the load, the fall zone under a suspended load).
- 1926.21 (safety training and instruction for each employee), for the first-day orientation angle.

## How the Lens lines up
- Zone boundaries drawn where the control line or guardrail belongs (1926.1424, 1926.501).
- Danger wording and red for immediate hazards, caution and yellow on approach (1926.200).
- A first-day worker is shown what the hazard areas are and where they start (the training duty in 1926.1424).
- Zones come down when the hazard is gone (1926.200).
- Wording to use: "supports" or "reinforces" these rules. Never "replaces barriers" or "ensures compliance".
